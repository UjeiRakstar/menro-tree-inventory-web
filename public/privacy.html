Green Tag · Province of Laguna

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026. Written under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

Who we are

Green Tag (greentag.laguna.gov.ph) is the tree inventory and biodiversity system of the Provincial Government of Laguna, run by the Provincial Government Environment and Natural Resources Office (PGENRO) with the Municipal and City Environment and Natural Resources Offices (MENRO / CENRO) of Laguna, and developed with Laguna State Polytechnic University. PGENRO is the personal information controller for the system.

What we collect, and from whom

Visitors

Looking at the map, the BioDashboard and the other public pages needs no account, and we collect nothing personal from it. We use no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics services.

Residents who sign in with Google

  • Your name as Google gives it, or the nickname you choose instead. The leaderboard shows only this.
  • The email address of your Google account, so the same person is counted once. It is shown to nobody, not even the offices.
  • The trees you tag: where each tree stands, its species, the photos you take of it, and whether a field officer has verified it.

Nothing else is taken from your Google account: not your contacts, your photos or your location.

People who write to an office through Contact Us

Your name, email address and message, and the address and affiliation if you give them. Only the office you choose reads them.

People who download the inventory

Your first and last name, email address, affiliation and the purpose you give, the place whose trees you asked for and which files, with your agreement, before the download. Only PGENRO reads them, to know who uses the inventory and for what.

Office accounts (PGENRO, MENRO / CENRO and their field officers)

Name, email address, designation, office address, contact number and photo; and a record of the changes made through the account, kept so the inventory can be audited.

Tree photos and inventories

Photos are of trees. Please do not photograph people. When an office brings in an existing inventory or map file, the system leaves out columns holding personal data, such as owners' names, before anything is saved.

Why we use it

  • To keep the province's inventory of trees and green spaces, and to verify what residents tag.
  • To count the residents who take part, as the City Biodiversity Index asks of each city and municipality, and to rank the leaderboard.
  • To let the offices answer the messages sent to them.
  • To run the office accounts securely, and to know who changed what.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it beyond what this page says.

Who else handles it for us

These services run parts of Green Tag under their own security and privacy terms. They handle the information only to provide their service.

  • Supabase: the database, the accounts and sign-in.
  • Google: the "Continue with Google" sign-in; and Gemini, which the field app and the offices use to suggest a tree's species from its photos.
  • Cloudinary: stores the photos of tagged trees.
  • Brevo: sends an office's email reply to a message, when the office replies by email.
  • Map providers (such as OpenStreetMap and Esri): draw the map. Your browser asks them for map pictures, as with any web map.

What the public sees

  • Trees, with their species, measurements and photos, so everyone can learn about the province's trees.
  • Trees tagged and not yet verified, shown grey so nobody tags one twice: where they are and what species, never who tagged them.
  • The leaderboard: names or nicknames and counts only.

How long we keep it

  • A resident's account: until you delete it. On the Community page, "Delete my account" removes your sign-in, email, name or nickname and agreement at once. The trees you tagged stay in the inventory, as trees, credited to nobody.
  • Messages: until the office that received them deletes them.
  • Data requests: until PGENRO removes them. Ask PGENRO through Contact Us to have yours removed.
  • Office accounts: kept, and archived when their holder leaves, so the records they made keep their author, as government records must.

How we protect it

Information travels encrypted (HTTPS). Every office sees only its own city or municipality's records, and the database itself enforces it. Access keys are kept on the server, never in your browser.

Your rights, and whom to ask

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to be told what we hold about you, to see it, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, and to object to its use. A resident can delete their own account at any time from the Community page. For anything else, write to:

PGENRO Laguna, which keeps Green Tag's records: use the Contact Us form on the About page and choose "PGENRO — data privacy request". It reaches PGENRO alone, marked as a privacy request.

If you believe your information was mishandled, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the new version is posted here with its date.
