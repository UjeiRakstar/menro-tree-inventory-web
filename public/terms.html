In short

  • Green Tag's maps, figures and reports are for planning purposes only. They are not a land title, a survey plan, proof of ownership, or a permit.
  • Figures such as carbon, growth and biodiversity scores are estimates, worked out from published methods and stated sources, for the trees the system has measured.
  • Species suggested by artificial intelligence can be wrong. A tree's record is confirmed by a field officer.
  • What you post — a tree tag, a message, a nickname — must be true and decent. The offices check it before it is shown.

Who runs Green Tag

Green Tag is the tree inventory and biodiversity system of the Provincial Government of Laguna, run by the Provincial Government Environment and Natural Resources Office (PGENRO) with the Municipal and City Environment and Natural Resources Offices (MENRO / CENRO) of Laguna, and developed with Laguna State Polytechnic University. By using the site you agree to these terms.

The maps and the figures

  • For planning purposes only. Boundaries, land cover, planting sites and hazard areas are drawn from the sources named on each map and may differ from what is on the ground. Do not use them to settle a boundary, a claim or a sale.
  • Hazard maps show exposure, not risk. They show how much of a place lies in a flood or landslide hazard level from UP NOAH's maps; they do not predict what will happen. In an emergency, follow PAGASA, PHIVOLCS and your local DRRM office.
  • Estimates are estimates. Carbon and growth come from published equations and Philippine rates (named on the BioDashboard and in each report), and speak only for the trees measured. The City Biodiversity Index points cover only the indicators the system can measure, not the whole index.
  • Data from other agencies — PSA, NAMRIA, DA–BSWM, UP NOAH, DOST-PAGASA, Copernicus, DENR-ERDB, OpenStreetMap and Esri — is theirs, used under their terms, and only as accurate as they made it.

Species information and identification

  • The species write-ups in the Tree Database are taken by PGENRO from the DENR Ecosystems Research and Development Bureau's RISE publications, with the source of each named. They are information, not professional advice for a specific tree.
  • Species suggested from photographs are made by an artificial intelligence service and may be mistaken. They are a starting point for the field officer, not a final identification.
  • For a tree that may fall, or before any cutting, ask your MENRO / CENRO. A cutting permit is issued by the office, not by this site.

What you post

  • Tree tags. Tag only trees you have seen, where they are, with your own photographs. A tag is checked by a field officer before it becomes part of the inventory, and may be corrected or declined.
  • Photographs. By sending a photograph you allow PGENRO and the offices to show it with the tree and use it in the inventory. Do not send photographs of people, or of private property beyond the tree.
  • Messages and nicknames. Be truthful and respectful. Nicknames that are lewd, abusive or that pretend to be an office are refused; a message or tag that is false, abusive or advertising may be removed.
  • Your account. Residents join with their own Google account, and may delete it at any time on the Community page. Office accounts are for the office they were issued to and may not be shared.

Using the data

The tagged trees may be downloaded after the data request form is filled in. You may use them for study, research, planning and education, citing "Green Tag, PGENRO Laguna" and the date downloaded, and keeping the sources the data names. Do not use them to identify or contact individual people, or present them as an official survey.

Links to other sites are for convenience; their content is their own.
